## Steps

T023/T029 conventions throughout: RL1, threshold 0.70, window [0.66, 0.70), bin width 0.01, polynomial degree 5, firm-cluster bootstrap with 999 draws and full re-estimation, fixed seed. Assert first that b_post = 3.14, b_pre = −0.88 and D = 0.1146 are reproduced (rounded).

1. **Direct test of the change in bunching.** Δb = b_post − b_pre with a joint firm-cluster bootstrap: each draw resamples firms once and re-estimates both periods on the same draw. Report Δb, SE, 95% CI and the share of draws with Δb > 0. The same for the placebo thresholds 0.60 and 0.80 and for the variants degree4, degree7, narrow and wide from t023. Table `t043_delta_b.csv/.tex`.
2. **Integration constraint (Chetty et al. 2011).** Iterate the counterfactual upward above 0.70 until the excess mass below equals the missing mass in [0.70, 0.74] (the counterfactual above is shifted until counts integrate). Report b, missing mass above, and CI, post and pre, next to the T023 baseline. If the iteration does not converge, report that and do not force a result. Table `t043_integration.csv/.tex`.
3. **Measurement-error simulation.** Error distribution: constructed minus published RL1 from `t040_matched_observations.csv` (ratio == "rl1", the matched pairs; record N). In each of 999 replications add an error drawn with replacement from this empirical distribution to every firm-date RL1 (post and pre), re-estimate b_post, b_pre and Δb. Report the mean and the 2.5/97.5 percentiles of the simulated estimates and the share of replications with b_post > 0 and with Δb > 0. Second variant: errors drawn only from pairs with published RL1 in [0.60, 0.80]. Table `t043_meas_error.csv/.tex`, figure `t043_meas_error.pdf` (histogram of simulated b_post with the baseline as a line).
4. **Pre-specified heterogeneity (descriptive).** b_post and D (official minus frozen compliance, as T029) with firm-bootstrap CIs, separately for:
   - (a) SOE vs. private (`soe_final`; firms without a final class excluded and counted);
   - (b) A-share vs. Hong Kong (`market`);
   - (c) number of the other two lines (RL2, RL3) met at 2020-06-30: both met vs. at least one breached.
   
   Cells with fewer than 30 firms are marked descriptive. Report N of firm-dates and unique firms beside each estimate. Table `t043_heterogeneity.csv/.tex`.
5. **Cash backing of minority-interest growth (pilot).** For each pilot firm and its window in `pilot_purposive.csv`: change in minority interests (from the panel) and the cash received from minority shareholders (`capital_from_nci`, from `nci_cashflow.csv`, summed over the window's reports; for HK firms the equivalent line as coded there), minus dividends paid to minority shareholders where coded. Ratio cash/ΔMI per firm and the pooled ratio for groups A and B. Where a value is missing, mark it and do not impute. Keep `pdf_page` for every cash value. Table `t043_cash_backing.csv/.tex`.
6. Report (`tasks/T043_report.md`): one short paragraph per check stating whether the core findings survive, without overclaiming; a table number → file → row filter/column for every figure the text will use.
