## Goal

T032 extracted, from the annual reports, where minority interests sit. This card uses the firms' own ad-hoc announcements to establish **who** supplied the new minority equity and **on what terms**. It is the direct evidence for Part III of the paper (STATE 2026-10-23): genuine partner equity, equity from a group affiliate, or financing with debt-like features. This is document coding, not analysis. Every coded field needs a page citation, and the PI verifies afterwards.

## Inputs (read only)

- `Daten/announcements/<firm_id>/*.pdf`, `Daten/announcements/manifest.csv` (279 announcements, 10 pilot firms)
- `replication/data/raw/manual/handcollect/nci_subsidiaries.csv`, `nci_cashflow.csv`, `registry_todo.csv` (from T032)
- `state/handcollect/PROTOCOL.md` (holder_type categories incl. `group_affiliate`; search terms), `state/handcollect/pilot_purposive.csv`

## Write scope

- `Daten/announcements/<firm_id>/txt/` (page-split text dumps)
- `replication/data/raw/manual/handcollect/announcements_triage.csv`, `transactions.csv`, `case_notes.md`, `extraction_log_T033.md`

## Steps

1. **Text dumps** per page, as in T032.
2. **Triage** (`announcements_triage.csv`, one row per announcement):
   - firm_id, date, title, file;
   - category ∈ {`subsidiary_capital_increase`, `new_investor_in_subsidiary`, `equity_transfer_or_disposal`, `cooperation_or_jv_agreement`, `guarantee`, `nonstandard_financing` (e.g. 购房尾款收益权融资, factoring, trust loans), `related_party_financing` (group finance company, loans from the controlling group), `perpetual_or_other_equity_instrument`, `irrelevant`};
   - a one-line reason.

   Code only from the document text.
3. **Transactions** (`transactions.csv`, one row per transaction in a relevant announcement):
   - firm_id, announcement date, file, pdf_page;
   - subsidiary (as printed), counterparty name (as printed);
   - holder_type per PROTOCOL: trust / asset_mgmt / insurer / bank_wmp / developer / group_affiliate / soe_local / other_corporate / individual / unknown;
   - relation to the listed firm as stated in the document (e.g. 控股股东, 关联方, 无关联关系);
   - amount and currency;
   - ownership before and after (%);
   - whether the listed firm keeps control/consolidation, as stated.
4. **Contract terms**, one column each, with values yes / no / not stated, plus page and a verbatim excerpt of at most 300 characters for every "yes":
   - repurchase or buy-back obligation (回购);
   - fixed or guaranteed return (固定收益/保底);
   - shortfall make-up (差额补足);
   - put option or redemption right;
   - guarantee by the listed firm for the subsidiary's obligations to the counterparty;
   - shareholder loans or the right to draw surplus funds (富余资金调拨), including the interest rule;
   - exit or deadline clauses (退出/期限).
5. **Linking:** match each transaction's subsidiary to `nci_subsidiaries.csv` and `registry_todo.csv` by name. Record the exact or normalized match, or "no match"; never guess. For matched subsidiaries, add the T032 NCI balance change between the two reports and the cash from NCI in the same period.
6. **`case_notes.md`:** per firm, a factual summary of at most 12 lines: which transactions explain the main change in minority interests, who the counterparties are, and which contract terms are documented. Every sentence carries its citation [file, p.]. No interpretation beyond the documents; mark open points as "not documented".
7. `extraction_log_T033.md`: scanned or unreadable files, ambiguous items, and anything not coded with the reason.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every coded value has file and pdf_page. The data reviewer spot-checks at least 25 values against the txt dumps, and they match verbatim.
2. No information from outside the 279 announcements and the T032 outputs. No guessed counterparties or terms.
3. The 华发 / 珠海市海川地产 capital increases (announcements of 2021-05-26, 2021-10-19 and 2022-03-29) are coded completely, as the benchmark case.
4. `verified_by_PI` column present and empty.
