## Rules

1. **Column spacing:** tables with four or more data columns, or with interval cells, distribute the free width evenly across all columns (`tabular*` with `\extracolsep{\fill}` inside `apptabular`, or `tabularx` with several `X` columns); at least 1em between any two columns. An estimate with its interval goes either in two columns (estimate | 95% CI) or stacked in one cell (interval on a second line in `\makecell`); never side by side in one narrow cell. Keep full width.
2. **Text and floats:** each subsection = one short paragraph that introduces its tables/figures in the order they appear, followed by those floats placed with `[H]` (package `float`) or `[!htbp]`; no standalone one-sentence paragraphs (merge them into the preceding paragraph or into the table note); remove `\FloatBarrier`s that create gaps; a large float may start a new page, but no page of an appendix section may be less than about 60 percent filled except the last page of a section.
3. **Figures:** width `\linewidth` (two-panel) or `0.8\linewidth` (single), height at most `0.45\textheight`, caption below with notes in `\floatnote`.
4. **Visual check:** after the build, render every appendix page (`pdftoppm -r 50`) into `review/T071_pages/`, look at each page, and report per page: fill estimate, any overflow, any run-together columns, any orphan text. Iterate until all pass.
5. **Do not change** numbers, row order, labels, captions' meaning, `\label`s or the main text; verify with a before/after extraction of all numbers and all caption/label strings.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every appendix page passes rule 2; no run-together columns; no overfull boxes > 1 pt; main text unchanged and ending on page 15.
2. Before/after check of numbers and caption/label strings: identical.
3. Report: table of appendix pages with fill estimate before and after.
