## Goal

Replaces version 1 (`state/T029_v1_superseded.md`), after an external review on 2026-10-23. Q2 is re-posed as a measurable question:

**How much of the post-policy improvement in reported RL1 compliance depends mechanically on minority-interest growth?**

This connects the two existing findings, bunching below 0.70 (T023) and minority-interest growth among crossers (T027), directly. It avoids selection on crossing by using all firms, classified by their predetermined end-2019 position. The results are mechanical accounting counterfactuals, not causal effects and not a measure of economic leverage.

## Steps

Main sample. N = tl − adv, E = te (total equity including mi), RL1 = N/(N+E). Use only rows with N > 0 and E > 0, and report exclusions. Firm-cluster bootstrap with 999 draws, firms resampled with full histories, everything re-estimated inside each draw (as in 06_bunching_tests.R). Seed fixed. All specifications are pre-specified; report all.

**Part A – counterfactual compliance (primary)**
1. For every firm-date t ≥ 2020-12-31, build two counterfactuals.
   - Frozen MI: R̃_it = N_it / (N_it + E_it − MI_it + MI_i,2019-12-31). Answers: "Would the firm satisfy RL1 if its minority interests had stayed at their end-2019 level, holding all other observed items at realized values?"
   - Ex-MI: R^B_it = N_it / (N_it + E_it − MI_it). Shows the stock-level dependence. Label it "mechanical, not a deconsolidated balance sheet".
   - Require MI_i,2019-12-31 observed. Flag firm-dates where MI changes by more than 50% of E in one half-year (possible consolidation changes) and show results with and without them.
2. Compliance shares by date, 2017H1–2022H2 (and the extension window separately, if available):
   - official C = 1(RL1 ≤ 0.70);
   - frozen C̃ = 1(R̃ ≤ 0.70);
   - ex-MI C^B.
   
   Key statistic: the share of officially compliant firm-dates that would fail under frozen MI, D̃ = C − C̃, post-policy. Pre-period placebo: freeze MI at 2016-12-31 and compute the same for 2017H2–2019H2. Firm-cluster bootstrap CIs for D̃ post, D̃ placebo, and their difference.
3. Bunching under the counterfactual: run the T023 excess-mass estimator (same bins, polynomial, window, bootstrap) on R̃ post-policy and compare with official RL1 post-policy (b = 3.14). Report the difference b_official − b_frozen with a firm-bootstrap CI using paired draws.
   - Interpretation rule, stated before the results: a large drop means the bunching depends mechanically on post-2019 minority-interest growth. No drop means the bunching is achieved through other items.
4. Debt benchmark: for the same firm-dates, report the change since end-2019 in interest-bearing debt (log, and scaled by end-2019 total assets) for officially compliant firms that fail under frozen MI, compared with firms compliant under both.

**Part B – adjustment by predetermined distance (all firms, no selection on crossing)**
5. g_i = RL1_i,2019-12-31 − 0.70. Bins: < −0.10, −0.10 to 0, 0 to 0.10, > 0.10. Sensitivity: g_i measured at 2019-06-30.
6. Outcomes per firm-date, all scaled by total assets at end-2019 (TA_i,2019): cumulative change since end-2019 in MI, in parent equity (te − mi), in ibd, and in N.
   Event-study style regression: Y_it = α_i + λ_t + Σ_k β_k^bin · 1(bin) · 1(t = k), with reference date 2019-12-31 and reference bin "< −0.10". Firm-clustered SE. Present it as differential adjustment by initial position, not as a policy effect. Show the pre-period coefficients.
   Figure: one panel per outcome, lines per bin.
7. Exact component contributions (as in T027) for all transitions of firms with 0 ≤ g_i < 0.10, pre vs. post. Report them in log-odds and in RMB-scaled terms (ΔX / TA_i,2019). Report medians and the firm-level distribution, plus a leave-one-firm-out range for the post median of the mi contribution.

**Part C – incentive heterogeneity (descriptive unless cells are large enough)**
8. Classify firms with RL1 > 0.70 at end-2019: `rl1_only` (RL2 and RL3 met at end-2019) vs. `rl1_plus`. First report the number of unique firms per group within RL1 ∈ [0.60, 0.80] pre and post.
   If both groups have at least 15 firms: post-minus-pre share in [0.68, 0.70), and D̃ by group, with firm-bootstrap CIs. Otherwise report descriptives only and say so.
   Notes:
   - The benefit B is the economic value of a relaxed cap and need not be larger for rl1_only. State this as an assumption about financing constraints, not a prediction.
   - Tier rules: cite the focal paper's description (`jin2025deleveraging`) only.
