## Data route actually used (PI, 2026-10-31/11-01)

EastMoney's industry-board endpoint (push2) refused script requests, so the control roster was built from **Sina Finance industry lists** (`ak.stock_sector_spot(indicator="新浪行业")`, `ak.stock_sector_detail`) for the pre-specified sectors 建筑建材, 水泥行业, 机械行业, 化工行业, 钢铁行业, 有色金属, 电力行业, 供水供气, 交通运输 and 煤炭行业 (coal added on 1 Nov to match the pre-specification); balance sheets come from the same EastMoney endpoint as the developer panel. Known developers excluded. Firms whose balance-sheet request lacks any of the requested periods failed (`balance_download_errors.csv`; largely firms not listed throughout 2016–2022 or long delisted), so the control panel is **balanced by construction** (all 14 dates). Consequences to state: (i) classification by Sina rather than EastMoney boards; (ii) no broad and no Hong Kong control; (iii) balanced, survivor-only control vs. unbalanced developer panel – therefore compute ΔΔb also against the **balanced developer panel** (T058 step 4, 115 firms) as a co-primary comparison; (iv) report the failure list by sector. Step 3's broad-control and Hong Kong variants are dropped; step 5 (SOE split) only if ownership can be merged from existing files – otherwise drop and say so.

## Steps

T023 conventions (window [0.66, 0.70), bins 0.01, degree 5 over [0.40, 1.00], 999 firm-bootstrap draws with re-estimation, fixed seed); pre 2017H1–2019H2, post 2020H2–2022H2. Assert reproduction of developer b_pre −0.88, b_post 3.14, Δb 4.02 first.

1. **Control panel:** RL1 = (total liabilities − advance receipts − contract liabilities) / (total assets − advance receipts − contract liabilities), exactly as for developers; same date set, same cleaning rules (document every rule applied); report N firm-half-years and firms per date and the share in [0.40, 1.00].
2. **Control bunching:** b_pre, b_post, Δb at 0.70 for the main control; placebo thresholds 0.60 and 0.80; histograms with fitted counterfactual (figure `t061_control_density.pdf`, pre and post panels).
3. **Difference-in-bunching:** ΔΔb = Δb_developers − Δb_control, bootstrap resampling firms within each group (stratified), 95 percent CI; also for A-share developers only vs. the A-share control (same-market comparison) and, if available, Hong Kong developers vs. Hong Kong control. Broad control as sensitivity. Pre-specified reading: ΔΔb > 0 with CI above zero means the post-policy concentration is specific to regulated developers; ΔΔb near zero means a general shift at 0.70 (reporting change or focal point) cannot be excluded.
4. **Power and support:** number of control firm-half-years in [0.66, 0.70) per period; if fewer than 30 in a period, flag the control estimate as low support.
5. **SOE warning lines:** state-owned enterprises face asset-liability warning lines since 2018; report Δb for SOE and non-SOE controls separately where ownership is available.
6. **κ figure fonts:** re-render `t055_kappa.pdf` from the existing T055 numbers with axis, legend and note text at least 9 pt at `\linewidth` in the paper (numbers unchanged; byte changes to this one figure allowed).
7. Report: one paragraph per step on what the result means; number → file → filter table.
