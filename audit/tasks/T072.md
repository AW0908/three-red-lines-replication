## Style (one theme for all)

- Font: serif (Times-like, e.g. `family = "serif"`), base size so that axis labels and tick labels are **≥ 9 pt at the printed width** in the PDF (check by computing printed size); titles inside the graphic are dropped (the LaTeX caption is the title); **no notes inside graphics** (move them to the `\floatnote`).
- Colours: course palette – primary green `#549E39`, accent `#6B9F25`, light `#D1DFCE`, dark `#3D7A28`, plus one neutral grey `#7F7F7F` and, where a second contrast is needed, a muted blue `#2F5D8A`; regulated region / threshold marked with a thin dashed dark line at 0.70.
- Consistent aspect ratios: single panel 6.5 × 4 in; two-panel 6.5 × 3.6 in; small multiples laid out to fill the grid (no lone panel on the last row: use 4 × 4 or 5 × 3, or drop to 12 dates with a separate panel for the rest).
- Legends below the plot, horizontal; axis labels in words ("Reported RL1", "Share of firm-half-years").
