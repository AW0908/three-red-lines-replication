## Goal

Answer the instructor's question: why does bunching at the threshold indicate management of the reported balance sheet rather than simple policy success? Genuine deleveraging shifts the whole distribution and also lowers *economic* leverage; targeting of the *reported* ratio creates excess mass only for the regulated definition, at reporting dates, often reverting afterwards and achieved through cosmetic items. Research question and definitions: `state/STATE.md` (2026-10-19).

## Steps

Main sample, pre-period 2017H1–2019H2, post-period 2020H2–2022H2 (same as T005). Seed fixed. Firm-clustered inference wherever a test statistic is reported.
1. **Placebo definitions:** pre/post shares in the bin just below and just above the threshold for rl1 vs rl1_alt (threshold 0.70, bin width 0.02) and rl2 vs rl2_adj (threshold 1.00, bin width 0.05). Difference-in-bunching: (post − pre share below threshold) for the regulated minus the placebo definition, with firm-cluster bootstrap CI (999 draws).
2. **Reporting date:** the same shares separately for 30 June and 31 December reporting dates in the post-period (and pre-period).
3. **Reversal:** among firm-dates in the post-period with rl1 in [0.68, 0.70), share with rl1 > 0.70 at the next reporting date; compare with firm-dates in [0.64, 0.68) and with the same transition in the pre-period. Same for rl2 in [0.95, 1.00).
4. **Composition:** for firm-dates entering [0.68, 0.70) from above 0.70 between t−1 and t (post-period), the change from t−1 to t in log ibd, cash/ta, mi_share, perp_te, jv_ta, log ta, compared with firm-dates that stay above 0.70 (means and firm-clustered difference tests). Report as descriptive.
5. **Excess-mass estimator (Kleven 2016):** for rl1, fit a polynomial (degree 5; robustness 4 and 7) to bin counts (width 0.01, range 0.40–1.00) excluding a window [0.66, 0.70) (robustness [0.67, 0.70) and [0.66, 0.72)); excess mass b = (observed − counterfactual in window)/counterfactual per bin; separately pre and post; firm-cluster bootstrap SE (999). Placebo thresholds 0.60 and 0.80. Also for rl1_alt as placebo definition.
6. Outputs: `t023_placebo_definitions.csv/.tex`, `t023_reporting_dates.csv`, `t023_reversal.csv/.tex`, `t023_composition.csv`, `t023_excess_mass.csv/.tex`, figures `t023_bunching_rl1_vs_alt.pdf` (overlaid pre/post histograms for rl1 and rl1_alt) and `t023_excess_mass_rl1.pdf` (observed vs counterfactual, post-period). TEX notes plain English, no unescaped underscores.
