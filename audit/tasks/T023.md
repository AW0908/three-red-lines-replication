## Goal

Answer the instructor's question: why does bunching at the threshold indicate management of the reported balance sheet rather than simple policy success? Genuine deleveraging shifts the whole distribution and also lowers *economic* leverage; targeting of the *reported* ratio creates excess mass only for the regulated definition, at reporting dates, often reverting afterwards and achieved through cosmetic items.
