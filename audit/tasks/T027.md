## Goal

Answer the "how" sub-question of the research question (`state/STATE.md`, 2026-10-19) directly. For firms whose regulated ratio falls below a red line, decompose the decline into its exact sources:
- less debt (real adjustment);
- more equity, split into parent equity, minority interests and perpetual instruments. Minority interests and perpetuals are cosmetic-leaning only under the measurement assumptions of the theory note.

Compare the decomposition of post-policy crossings with crossings in the pre-policy period, when there was no incentive to target 0.70 / 1.00. This needs no full economic-leverage measure (D20), and it conditions on firms that actually moved, so it has fewer power problems than the dose DiD.

## Steps

Main sample. Pre-period: transitions with t in 2017H1–2019H2. Post-period: t in 2020H2–2022H2 (same as T023). Transitions are between consecutive half-year dates t−1 → t of the same firm; no gaps bridged. Seed fixed. Firm-cluster bootstrap with 999 draws for every reported test.

1. **Identity checks first.** Verify on the panel that te (total equity) includes mi and that ta = tl + te holds up to rounding; report the share of rows violating |ta − tl − te|/ta > 0.01. Then:
   - RL1 = N/(N + te) with N = tl − adv.
   - Verify the algebra numerically: max abs difference to rl1.

2. **Events.**
   - Main: downward crossings of RL1, rl1_{t−1} ≥ 0.70 and rl1_t < 0.70.
   - Secondary: RL2, rl2_{t−1} ≥ 1.00 and rl2_t < 1.00.
   - Benchmark A: the same crossings in the pre-period.
   - Benchmark B, post-period only: firm-dates with rl1_{t−1} ≥ 0.70 that stay ≥ 0.70 ("stayers").
   - Report N of events and firms per cell.

3. **Exact decomposition of RL1 (log-odds).**
   - logit(RL1) = ln N − ln te, so Δlogit = Δln N − Δln te, exactly.
   - Split Δln N by item: Δln N × (ΔX/ΔN) for X = ibd and X = other non-advance liabilities (tl − adv − ibd). This is exact because the items sum to N.
   - Split Δln te the same way: X = parent equity excluding perpetuals (te − mi − perp), mi, perp. Perp is observed only where perp_missing is FALSE. Report the perp split only on that subsample and say so (D19).
   - Guard: where ΔN or Δte is 0, set the item shares to NA.

4. **Exact decomposition of RL2 (levels).**
   - ΔR = (N1 − N0)/D1 − N0(D1 − D0)/(D0·D1) with N = ibd − cash and D = te.
   - Split the numerator term into ibd and −cash, and the denominator term into parent equity, mi and perp, as in step 3.

5. **Summary per cell** (post crossings, pre crossings, post stayers).
   - Medians (primary) and means of each component.
   - Share of events with Δln ibd < 0 ("debt actually fell").
   - Share of events where the equity term contributes more than half of the decline in logit(RL1).
   - Share of the equity term coming from mi + perp.

6. **Tests** (firm-cluster bootstrap CIs):
   - post crossings minus pre crossings for: the median Δln N, the median Δln te, the share with ibd falling, and the mi+perp share of the equity term;
   - post crossings minus post stayers for Δln ibd.
   - Multiple testing: report all, star nothing.

7. **Absolute debt growth** (A-share firms only for RMB levels; HK within-firm log changes are currency-invariant and may be pooled; say which):
   - median Δln ibd for post crossers vs post stayers vs pre crossers;
   - share of post crossers whose ibd rose while RL1 fell.

8. **Robustness**:
   - narrow events: rl1_t in [0.66, 0.70) only;
   - exclude fye_flag firms;
   - winsorized means at the 1st and 99th percentiles;
   - two-period crossings: t−2 → t.

9. **Outputs**:
   - `t027_identity_checks.txt`
   - `t027_events.csv`
   - `t027_decomposition_rl1.csv/.tex`
   - `t027_decomposition_rl2.csv/.tex`
   - `t027_tests.csv/.tex`
   - `t027_debt_growth.csv`
   - `t027_robustness.csv`
   - figure `t027_decomposition_rl1.pdf`: stacked median contributions of ibd, other liabilities, parent equity and mi+perp to Δlogit(RL1), for post crossings, pre crossings and post stayers.

   TEX notes in plain American English with no unescaped special characters. They must state that mi and perp count as cosmetic-leaning only under the measurement assumptions of the theory note: debt-like partner equity, and perpetuals booked as equity.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Identity checks pass, or deviations are reported with counts. Decomposition components sum to the total change for every event, up to 1e-10; this is asserted in code with stopifnot.
2. Outputs reproducible across two runs (byte-identical; bootstrap seeded). Existing outputs byte-identical.
3. The report states, for each statistic, which value would indicate real vs. cosmetic adjustment and what the data show. Descriptive results are labeled as such. No claim that mi or perp are cosmetic without the stated assumption.
4. N per cell reported. If fewer than 20 post-period RL1 crossings exist, the worker stops after step 2 and reports.
