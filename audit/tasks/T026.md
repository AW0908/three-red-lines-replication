## Binding model specification (Co-PI; derive, do not change the assumptions)

Single regulated ratio, static, one developer.
- Pre-policy reported ratio R0; threshold τ; gap g = R0 − τ.
- Policy creates a notch: if the reported ratio R ≤ τ the developer obtains a discrete benefit B > 0 (higher allowed debt growth, 5 percentage points per line in the policy).
- Choices: real adjustment r ≥ 0 (lowers economic and reported leverage) and cosmetic adjustment c ∈ [0, c̄] (lowers only reported leverage). R = R0 − r − c; economic leverage L = R0 − r.
- Costs: C_r(r) = (α_r/2) r², C_c(c) = (α_c/2) c², α_r, α_c > 0; c̄ > 0 is the accounting limit on cosmetic adjustment.
- Payoff: B·1[R ≤ τ] − C_r(r) − C_c(c).

Results to state and prove (with all steps):
1. Firms with g ≤ 0 do not adjust.
2. Cost minimisation for a given gap g > 0: interior solution r* = g α_c/(α_r+α_c), c* = g α_r/(α_r+α_c) if c* ≤ c̄; otherwise c = c̄, r = g − c̄. Minimum cost K(g) = (1/2) g² α_r α_c/(α_r+α_c) in the interior case (and the corresponding expression in the corner case).
3. Compliance iff K(g) ≤ B; in the interior case iff g ≤ ḡ = sqrt(2B(α_r+α_c)/(α_r α_c)). Compliers choose R = τ exactly (no incentive to overshoot because costs are increasing); non-compliers with g > ḡ do not adjust at all.
4. Predictions: (P1) excess mass of the reported ratio at τ and missing mass in (τ, τ+ḡ]; with optimisation frictions (some firms unable to adjust; Kleven 2016) the missing mass is partial and excess mass appears just below τ in binned data. (P2) cosmetic share of adjustment c*/(r*+c*) = α_r/(α_r+α_c) in the interior case: higher when real adjustment is relatively costly. (P3) economic leverage of compliers L = τ + c* is heterogeneous and above τ, so a measure that undoes cosmetic adjustment (e.g. rl2_adj, rl1_alt) shows no mass point at τ – the placebo-definition test in T023. (P4) ownership: if SOEs have lower α_r (easier equity issuance, asset sales, state-linked buyers), their adjustment is more real and ḡ is larger (more compliers); the sign of the total response by ownership is ambiguous – consistent with the unsigned H4. (P5) a reported-ratio decline larger than the economic-leverage decline (R falls by g, L by r* < g) is the signature of cosmetic adjustment.
5. Discussion (half a page): limitations – static, one ratio (three lines and the tier structure as extension), deterministic, cosmetic adjustment possibly reversed after the reporting date (reversal test in T023), regulators may anticipate cosmetic adjustment.
