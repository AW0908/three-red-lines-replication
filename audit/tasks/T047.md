## Steps

1. **Firms and windows.** All firms in `crossers_post.csv` not in `pilot_purposive.csv`; window `date0`–`date1` with one year look-back for transactions; fiscal-year window for the magnitude as in T044 Revision 2 (from the fiscal year-end before `date0` to the fiscal year-end at or after `date1`).
2. **Identify and code** every equity transaction that created or enlarged minority interests (capital increases, equity transfers to minority holders, consolidated new JVs) from the announcements and the annual reports' tables of material non-wholly-owned subsidiaries. Apply the T044 rubric exactly as implemented in `16_pilot_magnitude.R` after Rework 3 (feature columns with file, page and excerpt; `not stated` if not mentioned; subtype only from features; no ID- or text-based exceptions; group rows into economic transactions by subsidiary and agreement chain). Firms without any usable announcement are coded U with the reason.
3. **Statements** for every transaction coded D, G, H or E (one factual claim per line, existing format) appended to `case_notes.md`; numerical statements separately from qualitative ones.
4. **Magnitude (`18_crossers_coding.R`)** for the new firms and pooled with the four pilot crossers (group A of the pilot): ΔMI, NCI profit, capital from NCI, dividends, residual, and capital attributed to D1/D2/D-unspecified/G/H/E/U vs. not attributed; verification labels by the PI rule of 2026-10-28. Tables `t047_coding.csv/.tex` (firm × category counts by economic transaction, compact) and `t047_magnitude.csv/.tex` (per firm and pooled over all 31 crossers), with the headline totals: capital from NCI attributable to a coded category vs. not attributable, and counts by category.
5. **Check sample:** all D, G and H rows plus 5 random U rows, checked against the PDF page.
6. **Report:** per firm a three-line summary; pooled results for all 31 crossers; list of statements awaiting PI verification (line numbers), split into numerical and qualitative; number → file → filter table; open questions.

## Acceptance criteria

4. Two runs of `18_crossers_coding.R` byte-identical; decomposition adds up for complete firms.
