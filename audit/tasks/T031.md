## Goal

Q1 (targeting) is the paper's anchor. The external review of 2026-10-23 named the likely referee attacks:
- repeated observations of the same firms;
- sensitivity of the counterfactual density;
- changing sample composition (defaults, delistings);
- an imperfect comparison group in the reversal test;
- the timing of [number withheld]H2.

T023 already resamples whole firms and re-estimates everything inside each draw. This card adds the remaining checks so that Q1 can be called strong, or weakened if it fails them.
