## Goal

Q1 (targeting) is the paper's anchor. The external review of 2026-10-23 named the likely referee attacks:
- repeated observations of the same firms;
- sensitivity of the counterfactual density;
- changing sample composition (defaults, delistings);
- an imperfect comparison group in the reversal test;
- the timing of 2020H2.

T023 already resamples whole firms and re-estimates everything inside each draw. This card adds the remaining checks so that Q1 can be called strong, or weakened if it fails them.

## Steps

All with the T023 estimator: same seed, firm bootstrap with 999 draws, re-estimation inside each draw. Assert reproduction of the T023 main estimate (b_post = 3.14) first.
1. **Unique-firm support:** for the bunching window [0.66, 0.70), pre and post:
   - the number of firm-dates;
   - the number of distinct firms;
   - the number of distinct firms entering the window post-policy;
   - the maximum number of firm-dates contributed by one firm.
2. **Leave-one-firm-out:** re-estimate b_post dropping each firm with at least one firm-date in [0.60, 0.80]. Report min, max, and the five most influential firms (firm id and Δb).
3. **Sample composition:**
   - (A) all observations (as T023);
   - (B) balanced: firms with RL1 observed at all 13 dates;
   - (C) firms observed at least 3 times both pre and post.
   
   Report b_pre, b_post and the CI per sample.
4. **Sensitivity grid**, pre-specified and reported in full:
   - bin width ∈ {0.005, 0.01, 0.02};
   - excluded window ∈ {[0.67,0.70), [0.66,0.70), [0.65,0.70), [0.66,0.72)};
   - polynomial degree ∈ {3, 5, 7};
   - fitting range ∈ {[0.40,1.00], [0.50,0.90]}.
   
   One figure: b_post with CI per specification, sorted by specification index (not by size), with the T023 baseline marked. Report the share of specifications with CI excluding 0.
5. **Reversal by exact distance:** for firm-dates with RL1 ∈ [0.60, 0.70), regress 1(RL1 > 0.70 at next date) on a flexible function of the distance to 0.70 (bins of 0.01, plus a linear-in-distance version), interacted with post. Use a linear probability model with firm-clustered SE. Report the post − pre difference at distances 0.005, 0.015 and 0.025, plus a figure of predicted reversal probability by distance, pre vs. post.
6. **2020H2 timing:**
   - (i) post = 2020H2–2022H2 (T023 baseline);
   - (ii) post = 2021H1–2022H2, with 2020H2 excluded as announcement period;
   - (iii) 2020H2 alone.
   
   Report b and the reversal difference for each.
