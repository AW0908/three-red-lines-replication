## Goal

External feedback, priority 1–2 (2026-10-27): the whole analysis rests on RL1 being measured as the regulator measures it. The rules were never published as an official document. Rating agencies and the press report only the thresholds (e.g. S&P Global China Ratings, 28 Oct 2020), so the best available evidence is the ratio that **firms themselves published** for 2020–2021. This card codes those values, measures our error near 0.70, records how each firm treats perpetual bonds and advance receipts, and re-runs the main results under the firms' convention.

## Steps

1. **Coding**, one row per firm × date × ratio. Read each candidate snippet, plus the full page if the snippet is unclear. Record:
   - the published RL1, net gearing and cash-to-short-term-debt values with their reference date;
   - the verbatim definition wording if given (numerator, denominator, treatment of perpetual securities and contract liabilities);
   - the page.

   Discard values that refer to other ratios (plain liability-to-asset ratio, interest-coverage, etc.) and log why. No values from outside the documents.
2. **Comparison** (13_rl1_validation.R): merge with panel rl1/rl2/rl3 at the same date. Report:
   - the error distribution (constructed − published): mean, median, MAE, share within 0.5 and 1 pp;
   - the same for firms with published RL1 in [0.60, 0.80];
   - classification agreement at 0.70 (2 × 2 table);
   - a list of all disagreements with reasons where the document states the definition (perpetuals as debt, minority interests, other).
3. **Perpetual convention:** build rl1_perp = (tl − adv + perp)/(ta − adv) where perp is observed (D19). Report how often rl1 vs. rl1_perp changes the side of 0.70.
4. **Key results re-run, as robustness only:**
   - (a) T023 post-policy excess mass with rl1_perp (where perp is missing, rl1);
   - (b) T029 frozen-MI counterfactual bunching and D with rl1_perp;
   - (c) both main results restricted to firms whose published RL1 matches ours within 1 pp.

   Firm-cluster bootstrap with 999 draws, as in T023/T029.
5. Outputs:
   - `t040_validation.csv/.tex`: error summary and 2 × 2 classification table;
   - `t040_disagreements.csv`;
   - `t040_robustness.csv/.tex`;
   - figure `t040_constructed_vs_published.pdf`: scatter with 45° line, 0.70 lines, labels for disagreements.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every coded value has file and page. The data reviewer spot-checks 25 values against the PDFs.
2. Main estimates reproduce T023 (3.14) and T029 (D 0.1146) before any variation (assert).
3. The report states plainly whether the validation supports our RL1 near 0.70, and how the main results change under rl1_perp and the matched-firm sample.

## Stop conditions

- Fewer than 15 firms with a published RL1 → report the coverage, do steps 3–4(a, b) only.
