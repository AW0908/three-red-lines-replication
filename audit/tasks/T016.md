## Syllabus requirements (MTPA_Syllabus_Autumn26-27.pdf, p. 5–6)

- First draft 12–15 pages; final 15–20 pages.
- 1.5 line spacing, 1-inch margins, 12pt Times New Roman.
- Title page with abstract of at most 100 words (may be single spaced) and 3–5 keywords.
- Sections in this order: Introduction (research question + method, motivation, contribution in at most the first three paragraphs), Related literature (incl. focal paper), Theoretical model or framework (graph if possible), Empirical model (equations, identification, confounders), Context and data (institutional background, data sources, summary statistics, descriptive table), Preliminary analysis.
- Bibliography single spaced.
- AI-use appendix (tool, dates, purposes, representative prompts/tasks, how checked, how modified/rejected).
- Final version: changes since the first submission must be tracked or marked; the response to reviewers is a separate file.
