## Goal

Three independent referee reports (86, 82, 70/100, 30 Oct) agree that (i) "post" is calendar time rather than regulatory exposure, (ii) the minority-interest (MI) result may be mechanical for any equity item, and (iii) the bunching estimate needs attrition, balanced-panel and specification checks on Δb rather than on the level b. All computations below are pre-specified; report every result whatever it shows.

2. **Notch-intensity test (pre-specified).** Theory: crossing RL1 moves a firm up a full tier only if the other two lines are met (largest debt-growth gain). Estimate Δb (not only b_post) for firms meeting both other lines vs. breaching at least one (classification by the other two lines at the same date, as in `t043_heterogeneity.csv`), and the difference in Δb with a joint firm bootstrap and its 95 percent CI. 3. **Symmetric freezing benchmarks** (referee: any equity item that grew would "matter"). On the paired post sample, recompute compliant share, D and b when instead of MI one freezes at end-2019 (a) equity attributable to parent shareholders (`te − mi`), (b) interest-bearing debt (`std + ltd + notes + lease`, as defined in the panel; document the exact columns), (c) MI and parent equity jointly. Report alongside the MI row and normalise: D per unit of median growth of the frozen item since end-2019 (percentage points of compliance per percentage point of total assets). Same for the placebo anchor (end-2016). Pre-specified reading: MI is special if its D per unit of growth exceeds that of parent equity. Table `t058_symmetric.csv/.tex`.
4. **Balanced panel and attrition for Δb.** (a) Δb (pre and post) on the firms observed at every date from 2016-12-31 to 2022-12-31, and on firms observed at every pre and every post date of the main window; (b) attrition by bin: for firms that stop reporting after 2021-06-30, the distribution of their last observed RL1 (0.05 bins over [0.40, 1.00]) vs. continuing firms, with a test of equal shares just below 0.70 ([0.66, 0.70)); (c) Δb with exiting firms' last observation carried forward (bound). Table `t058_attrition.csv/.tex`, figure `t058_attrition.pdf`.
5. **Sample window.** Document why 2020-06-30 is excluded from pre and post (read `06_bunching_tests.R` and the README; quote the rule) and report b at 2020-06-30 alone and Δb with 2020-06-30 added to the pre period. Table `t058_window.csv`.
6. **Missing mass above 0.70.** For post and pre, the excess of observed over counterfactual counts in [0.70, 0.74) and [0.70, 0.80) (normalised as b), with CIs. Pre-specified reading: no missing mass above the threshold is consistent with imprecise ratio control or a diffuse response and is stated as such. Table `t058_above.csv/.tex`.
7. **Compact specification grid for Δb.** Using the grid in `t031_grid.csv` (bin width, exclusion window, polynomial degree, fit range), compute Δb (not b) for every specification; report the share with Δb > 0, the share with CI above zero, and min/median/max. One-page appendix table `t058_grid.tex` (summary by dimension) and `t058_grid.csv` (all rows), figure `t058_grid.pdf` (specification curve).
