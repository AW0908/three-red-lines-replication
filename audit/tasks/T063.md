## Steps

T023/T061 conventions unless stated (bins 0.01, degree 5 over [0.40, 1.00], 999 firm-bootstrap draws, fixed seed). Assert developer Δb 4.02 and ΔΔb 4.59 / 4.48 first.

1. **Notch with period-end noise (theory calibration).** Model: a firm that targets the threshold chooses an intended ratio r* just below 0.70 (target buffer β ≥ 0), but the reported ratio is r = r* + ε, with ε the period-end control error. Calibrate ε nonparametrically from the empirical distribution of (constructed − published) RL1 in `t040_matched_observations.csv` (RL1 rows; report N, mean, SD, quantiles; also a normal fit). Starting from the pre-policy developer distribution as the no-policy density, let a share π of firms within a band [0.70, 0.70 + Δ] (Δ ∈ {0.05, 0.10}) move to r* = 0.70 − β (β ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.02}) and add ε. For each (π, β, Δ) simulate the post distribution (1,000 replications, fixed seed), estimate b with the main estimator and the excess/missing mass in [0.70, 0.74). Report which parameter combinations reproduce the observed post b (3.14) **and** the observed excess above 0.70 (t058_above: 1.75 with [0.66, 0.74) excluded), and the implied predicted shape (figure `t063_noise_shapes.pdf`: observed post histogram vs. simulated mean histograms for 3–4 representative parameter sets; frictionless case ε = 0 included). Pre-specified reading: if plausible noise from the validation errors generates excess mass on both sides, the observed shape is consistent with targeting under imprecise control; if not, say so. Table `t063_noise.csv/.tex`.
2. **Co-main exclusion window [0.66, 0.74):** b_pre, b_post, Δb for developers (all, balanced, A-share, Hong Kong), control Δb, ΔΔb (all, balanced, A-share) with the fit excluding [0.66, 0.74) and mass measured in [0.66, 0.70) (and, separately, total mass in [0.66, 0.74)). Table `t063_window.csv/.tex`.
3. **Control without developer-supplier industries:** drop the Sina industries 建筑建材, 水泥行业 and 钢铁行业 (construction, cement, steel) from the control; report control Δb and ΔΔb (all, balanced, A-share), main and [0.66, 0.74) window. Also the reverse (only these three industries) as a descriptive check. Table `t063_control_sens.csv/.tex`.
4. **One observation per firm and distinct firms:** number of distinct firms with at least one observation in [0.66, 0.70), pre and post, developers and control; Δb and ΔΔb with one randomly drawn observation per firm and period (999 random draws of the observation, report median and 2.5–97.5 percentiles; seed fixed). Table `t063_one_obs.csv/.tex`.
5. **Distributions by half-year:** RL1 histograms (0.02 bins over [0.50, 0.90]) for developers and control at each date 2016H2–2022H2 (small multiples, figure `t063_by_date.pdf`) and the share of observations in [0.66, 0.70) and [0.70, 0.74) by date and group (`t063_by_date.csv`).
6. **Number reconciliation (no re-estimation):** (a) 1,692 firm-half-years vs. RL1 N = 1,691 – identify the observation and the reason; (b) CNKI 52 records vs. 51 journal articles – read `prisma_counts.csv` and state the definitions; (c) A-share developer Δb CI [−0.41, 5.41] (T061 bootstrap) vs. [−0.38, 5.30] (T058 bootstrap) and balanced Δb CI [1.48, 6.86] vs. [1.63, 6.99] – confirm both from their draws and state which bootstrap each table uses; (d) control Δb at 0.70 (−0.574) and 0.60 (−0.571) – confirm they are distinct estimates. Table `t063_reconcile.csv`.
7. Report: one paragraph per step on what it means; number → file → filter table.
