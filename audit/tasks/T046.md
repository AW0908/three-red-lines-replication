## Goal

Extend the T043 cash-backing check from the ten pilot firms to every firm in the main sample: for 2019, 2020 and 2021, how much of the change in minority interests (MI) was paid in as cash by minority shareholders, and does this differ between firms that crossed 0.70 through MI and other firms, and before and after the policy. Purpose: separate paid-in outside or group capital from consolidation and profit effects for the whole sample, not only the pilot. Descriptive; no causal claim.

- `replication/data/processed/panel_firm_halfyear.csv` (mi, ni_mi, rl1, sample_main)

## Steps

1. **Extraction.** From each FY2020 and FY2021 annual report in the manifest, take from the consolidated cash-flow statement the current-year and the comparative-year values of:
   - A-shares: 吸收投资收到的现金; 其中：子公司吸收少数股东投资收到的现金; 其中：子公司支付给少数股东的股利、利润;
   - HK: capital contributions / injections from non-controlling interests (or equivalent wording); dividends paid to non-controlling interests.
   
   The FY2020 report gives 2020 and 2019 (comparative), FY2021 gives 2021 and 2020; where both give 2020, use the FY2021 comparative only if FY2020 is missing and record any difference. One row per firm × year × metric with file, physical page, printed page, the verbatim line as excerpt, unit as printed, value in RMB, and `extraction_check` = `exact` if the value string appears verbatim on the cited page (checked by code), otherwise `manual`. A printed dash is 0 (noted); an absent line is `not reported` (not 0); a missing report is `report missing`.
2. **Cross-check** against the ten pilot firms in `nci_cashflow.csv`: list every difference.
3. **Analysis (`17_cash_backing_all.R`).** Per firm and year (2019, 2020, 2021): ΔMI from the panel (fiscal year-end to year-end), NCI profit (annual `ni_mi`), capital from NCI, dividends to NCI, residual = ΔMI − profit + dividends − capital. Report, for the main sample and pooled with sums (ratio of sums, not mean of ratios):
   - (a) capital from NCI / ΔMI and residual / ΔMI by year, for firms with ΔMI > 0;
   - (b) the same for the 31 post-policy MI crossers (`crossers_post.csv`) vs. all other firms, 2020–2021;
   - (c) 2019 (pre-policy) vs. 2020–2021;
   - (d) share of firm-years with ΔMI > 0 in which capital from NCI is at least 50 percent of ΔMI.
   
   Firm-cluster bootstrap CIs (999 draws, fixed seed) for the pooled ratios in (a)–(c). Firms with any missing component are excluded from the ratio of that year and counted. HK values: state the currency of the panel MI and of the cash-flow line; use only firm-years in which both are RMB (or convert with a documented rule if the report states another currency) and count exclusions.
4. Tables `t046_cash_backing_all.csv/.tex` (compact: rows = groups/years, columns = N firms, ΔMI, capital share, residual share, CI), `t046_extraction_audit.csv` (coverage: reports found, lines found, exact/manual), figure `t046_cash_share.pdf` (capital share by year, crossers vs. others).
5. Report: coverage, cross-check, results per (a)–(d), number → file → filter table, what remains unclear.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every value has file, physical page and excerpt; `extraction_check` computed by code; coverage table complete.
2. Pilot cross-check documented; differences explained or listed.
3. Two runs of `17_cash_backing_all.R` byte-identical; existing outputs unchanged.
4. Ratios are ratios of sums; exclusions counted; no imputation.
5. Verification rule: values with `extraction_check = exact` count as level strings (PI decision 2026-10-29, see Co-PI feedback); `manual` values are listed for the PI.
