## Goal

External feedback, priorities 4–5 (2026-10-27). Show that (1) the excess mass below 0.70 appears when the policy arrives, is not produced by repeated observations of the same firms, and is not an artifact of a flexible density, and (2) the post-2019 minority-interest dependence is unusual relative to placebo windows of the **same horizon**.

## Steps

All with the T023/T029 conventions: firm-cluster bootstrap with 999 draws and re-estimation, fixed seed. Assert reproduction of b_post = 3.14 and D = 0.1146 first.
1. **Bunching by half-year:** for each date 2017H1–2022H2, the share of firm-dates in [0.66, 0.70) relative to the counterfactual from the pooled pre-period polynomial (degree 5, same window). Report each date's ratio observed/counterfactual with a firm-bootstrap CI. Figure with a vertical line at Aug 2020. Report whether the jump is abrupt at 2020H2/2021H1.
2. **One observation per firm:**
   - (a) a single cross-section per date (2019H2, 2020H2, 2021H1, 2021H2, 2022H1) with excess mass and CI; cells under 60 firms are descriptive;
   - (b) 999 random draws of one post-policy observation per firm, giving the distribution of b and the share of draws with b > 0.
3. **Low-flexibility densities:**
   - polynomial degree 1 and 2 on a narrow fitting range [0.55, 0.85];
   - a local linear density fit outside the window (on bin counts);
   - the T023 baseline next to them.
   
   Report b and CI for each.
4. **Frozen-MI placebos with matched horizons:**
   - post: anchor 2019-12-31, evaluation dates 2020H2–2022H2, horizons 1–3 years;
   - placebos: anchors 2016-12-31 and 2017-06-30, evaluation dates 2017H2–2019H2 with the same horizon pattern as far as data allow.
   
   Compute D per horizon (1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3 years after the anchor) for post and placebo, and their differences with paired firm-bootstrap CIs. Figure: D by horizon, post vs. placebo.
5. Outputs:
   - `t041_bunching_by_date.csv/.tex`, `t041_one_per_firm.csv/.tex`, `t041_low_flex.csv/.tex`, `t041_frozen_horizons.csv/.tex`;
   - figures `t041_bunching_by_date.pdf`, `t041_frozen_horizons.pdf`.
