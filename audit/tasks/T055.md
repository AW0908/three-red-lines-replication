## Steps

T023/T029 conventions (window [0.66, 0.70), bins 0.01, degree 5 over [0.40, 1.00], firm bootstrap 999 draws with re-estimation, fixed seed). Assert reproduction of b_post = 3.14, D = 0.1146 and the paired sample (602 obs, 129 firms) first.

1. **Density-free measure of MI dependence** (referee: D rises mechanically with density near 0.70). For each paired observation, the MI-induced ratio shift s_it = R̃_it − R_it (frozen minus reported RL1, in percentage points of RL1). Report mean and median s for post (anchor end-2019) and placebo (anchor end-2016, window 2017H2–2019H2), their difference with a paired firm bootstrap, also restricted to firms with reported RL1 in [0.60, 0.80]. Second variant: the placebo D reweighted to the post-period distribution of reported RL1 over [0.60, 0.80] in 0.02 bins (inverse-probability weights by bin), and post minus reweighted placebo. Table `t055_mi_shift.csv/.tex`.
2. **κ-reclassification counterfactual** (referee: freezing deletes equity; the theory's κ is never used). For κ ∈ {0, 0.1, …, 1}, reclassify the share κ of MI growth since end-2019 (ΔMI_it = MI_it − MI_i,2019H2, only where ΔMI_it > 0) from equity to debt: R_it(κ) = (N_it + κ·ΔMI_it) / (N_it + E_it). Report compliant share and excess mass b(κ) with firm-bootstrap CIs, and the break-even κ* at which b(κ) first falls to zero and at which its CI first includes zero (interpolated; report if never). Same for the placebo anchor. Table `t055_kappa.csv/.tex`, figure `t055_kappa.pdf` (b and compliant share against κ, post vs. placebo).
3. **Falsification on the unadjusted ratio** (referee: a 70 percent focal point exists for the raw liabilities-to-assets ratio, used in SOE leverage warnings). Bunching b and Δb at 0.70 for the raw ratio tl/ta (total liabilities / total assets from the panel columns `tl` and `ta`, no deduction of advance receipts; note that `rl1_alt` is a different measure and is not used here) with identical settings, pre and post, and their difference from the RL1 results with a joint bootstrap. Pre-specified reading: excess mass at 0.70 for RL1 but not (or much less) for the unadjusted ratio supports targeting of the regulated definition; similar excess for both points to a common 70 percent focal point. Report both whatever the result. Table `t055_falsification.csv/.tex`.
4. **Linear MI trend** (referee: exponential extrapolation only). As T043 step 7 but extrapolating pre-policy MI linearly in levels (firm OLS slope over 2016-12-31 to 2019-12-31; same fallback rule). Report D_trend and b_trend. Table `t055_linear_trend.csv/.tex`.
5. **Pre-trend test table for the appendix:** collect the controlled event-study pre-trend test (p = 0.0002) and the other pre-trend tests from `t009_pretrend_tests.csv` and `t028_pretrend_controls.csv` into one compact table `t055_pretrend_tests.tex` (copy values; no re-estimation).
6. Report: one paragraph per step stating what the result means, without overclaiming; number → file → filter table.
