## Goal

The main DiD (`y ~ dose_pre:post | firm_id + date`) has no controls. Exposure to the red lines (dose_pre) is correlated with firm size, profitability, liquidity and business model, and these may have their own post-[number withheld] trends (e.g., the [number withheld]–[number withheld] sales collapse hit large, inventory-heavy developers hardest). Add **pre-determined** firm characteristics, measured before the policy, interacted with time. Contemporaneous controls are "bad controls" because the policy itself moves them. Then show how the dose coefficient changes. Section [number withheld] of the proposal will justify the choice.
