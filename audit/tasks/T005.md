## Goal

The descriptive evidence the syllabus requires for "Context and Data" and the first figures for "Preliminary
Analysis": summary statistics, the descriptive table by pre-policy treatment intensity (`dose`), the distribution of
the three red-line ratios before and after the policy (bunching at the thresholds) and breach shares over time.
Script `replication/code/03_figures.R` (step 3 of the replication package; reads only the processed panel).

## Steps

All on `sample_main == TRUE` unless stated. Periods: `pre` = dates 2017-06-30 … 2019-12-31; `post` = 2020-12-31 … 2022-12-31
(2020-06-30 is the transition date and belongs to neither window in steps 3–4).

1. **Summary statistics** (`t005_summary_stats`): for `rl1`, `rl2`, `rl2_adj`, `rl3`, `n_breached`, `mi_share`, `mi_gap`,
   `jv_ta`, `perp_te`, `rl2_gap`, `inv_ta`, `log_ta` (A-shares only for `log_ta`, HK currency unresolved): N, mean, SD,
   p10, p25, median, p75, p90 – for the full period and separately pre/post. Report percentiles; do not winsorise.
2. **Table by dose** (`t005_by_dose`): one column per `dose` = 0, 1, 2, 3 at 2019-12-31 (firm level): number of firms,
   share HK, share delisted by 2022, share `soe` = 1 (among known; also report the number with `soe` known), and the
   2019-12-31 mean and median of `rl1`, `rl2`, `rl3`, `mi_share`, `jv_ta`, `perp_te`, `log_ta` (A only).
   Add a column "All". LaTeX: booktabs, fragment for `\input` (no `\documentclass`), table notes as a `threeparttable` note
   naming the sample and the definition of `dose`.
3. **Bunching histograms**, one PDF per ratio: overlaid or side-by-side histograms pre vs. post (shares, not counts),
   vertical line at the threshold. Bins of equal width aligned so that the threshold is a bin edge:
   `rl1` width 0.02 on [0.30, 1.10]; `rl2` width 0.05 on [−0.50, 3.00]; `rl3` width 0.05 on [0, 3.00].
   Observations outside the plotted range are not dropped silently: report their number per period in the figure note
   and in `t005_bunching_counts.csv`. `t005_bunching_counts.csv`: per ratio × period × bin: count and share; plus per
   ratio × period the share of observations in the two bins just below and just above the threshold.
4. **Breach shares over time** (`t005_breach_shares.pdf`): for each of the 13 dates the share of firms with `b1`, `b2`,
   `b3` = 1 and the share with `n_breached` = 0; vertical line between 2020-06-30 and 2020-12-31 (policy, Aug 2020).
5. Figures: `ggplot2`, `theme_minimal`, PDF 6.5 × 4 in, axis labels in English, readable in black and white.
   Add `% source:` comments are not needed in the outputs; the README of the replication package lists the files later.
6. No regressions and no statistical tests in this task.
