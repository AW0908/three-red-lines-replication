## Ziel

Test whether sources other than annual reports and announcements reveal the terms of new minority equity (buyback,
guaranteed return, make-up, put/redemption, issuer guarantee, shareholder loans, exit) for the amount-ranked T079 sample,
and whether AI-assisted extraction scales the hand coding without lowering accuracy. Deliverable: a source-coverage table
(per source type: documents found, transactions matched, terms classifiable) and a verified extraction ledger. Purpose:
final paper (Finding [number withheld], disclosure argument); not for the Nov [number withheld] talk. No prevalence estimate of hidden debt, no causal claim.
