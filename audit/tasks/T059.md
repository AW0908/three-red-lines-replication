## Points

T058 results to state plainly (values after T058 Rework 1; re-read the CSVs): pilot vs. non-pilot Δb is uninformative (only four pilot firms in the post window; difference −1.53, wide CI) – one sentence, no conclusion. The pre-specified notch-intensity contrast is not supported (−1.30, CI includes zero) – say so in 6.1 and keep the prediction in 3.1 as pre-specified. Symmetric benchmark: after the policy, MI's D per unit of growth exceeds parent equity's (difference 10.0, CI above zero); the post-minus-placebo difference is imprecise (8.7, CI includes zero) – consistent with H2a, not with H2b. All 72 grid specifications give Δb > 0 (69 with CI above zero; median 3.9 in 0.01-bin units). Balanced-panel Δb 3.91; exits not concentrated below 0.70. **Mass above 0.70:** with the fit excluding [0.66, 0.74), there is also excess (not missing) mass just above the threshold after the policy (1.75, CI above zero; pre 0.85, CI includes zero): post-policy concentration is around 0.70, not only just below it. State this in 6.1 and in the theory (3.1): with imprecise control of a ratio that is reported only at period ends, firms aiming at the threshold land on both sides of it; the absence of missing mass above means the result is threshold-oriented concentration, not a clean notch response. Do not hide this. Standard transition: Hong Kong filers (no change inside the window) Δb 6.74, 8.61 without imputed-zero advance receipts; A-shares 2.10 (CI includes zero) – the concentration is not an artefact of the A-share standard change. Liu (2025) uses Shanghai and Shenzhen listings only; mention that my sample adds Hong Kong-listed developers, where concentration is strongest.
