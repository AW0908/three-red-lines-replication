## Goal

External probe review of 2026-10-28, weakness 2 and recommendation E4: show not only that debt-like terms exist but how large they are relative to the growth of minority interests. Result: for all ten pilot firms of `state/handcollect/pilot_purposive.csv`, (1) a coding of every equity transaction that created or enlarged minority interests in the firm's window, by a fixed rubric with page references, and (2) a decomposition of the change in minority interests into profits, dividends, cash contributions by category, and an unexplained residual. ## Rubric (fixed before coding; apply it, do not adapt it to the results)

Each transaction gets exactly one category, decided in this order:

1. **D – debt-like:** at least one of an investor put, a mandatory buyback or redemption by the issuer side, a fixed or guaranteed return, shortfall make-up (差额补足) by the issuer for the investor, or an exit at a pre-set price or date that does not depend on project value.
2. **G – group equity:** none of D, and the minority participant belongs to the issuer's own group (same controlling shareholder or actual controller, related party as stated in the document).
3. **H – hybrid:** none of D or G, and at least one of an issuer call option, priority or preferred dividends, a profit target, an exit valued at market or appraised value, or substantial loans from the minority partner to the project company.
4. **E – outside equity:** none of the above; an unrelated partner shares assets, risks, profits and losses pro rata.
5. **U – unclear:** the documents do not allow a decision (state what is missing).

Each feature column in `pilot_coding.csv` holds `yes`, `no` or `not stated`, with PDF file and physical page for every `yes` and `no`. A feature not mentioned is `not stated`, never `no`.

## Steps

1. **Identify the transactions.** For each pilot firm and its window `date0`–`date1` (one year before `date0` included as look-back): select from `transactions.csv` all equity transactions (capital increases, equity transfers to or from minority holders, new JVs that are consolidated). Check `announcements_triage.csv` and `contract_hits.csv` for equity transactions that T033 missed; add them from the PDFs. Record per firm which subsidiaries in `nci_subsidiaries.csv` show the largest NCI increase and whether a transaction covers them.
2. **Code** each transaction by the rubric into `pilot_coding.csv`: transaction_id (T033 id or new id T044-<firm>-<nn>), firm_id, date, file, page, subsidiary, partner, partner relation, amount of minority contribution (RMB, with unit as in the document), ownership before/after, all feature columns with pages, category, one-line reason. Amounts that are only proposed are marked `proposed`.
3. **Write statements** into `case_notes.md` under the firm's heading, one factual claim per line in the existing format `… [file, p. N]`, covering every transaction coded D, G, H or E and the decisive feature. Statements are for verification by the PI; write them so that each can be checked against one passage. No interpretation in the statements.
4. **Magnitude (`16_pilot_magnitude.R`).** Per firm and **fiscal-year window** (see Co-PI feedback, revision 2): ΔMI from the panel; NCI share of profit (`ni_mi`, summed over the window); dividends to NCI and cash contributions from NCI (`nci_cashflow.csv`); residual = ΔMI − profit + dividends − cash. Allocate cash contributions to the categories D/G/H/E/U where a coded transaction's paid amount matches a reporting period; the rest is "cash not attributed". Report per firm and pooled for groups A and B: shares of ΔMI by category, profit, residual. Every figure carries the verification state of its underlying statements (`verified` = all statements status yes and level meaning in `case_notes_verification.csv`; otherwise `unverified`). Tables `t044_pilot_coding.csv/.tex` (compact: firm × category counts and amounts) and `t044_magnitude.csv/.tex`.

6. **Report (`tasks/T044_report.md`):** per firm a three-line summary (transactions, categories, magnitude); a list of statements awaiting PI verification with their line numbers in `case_notes.md`; the number → file → filter table; what remains unclear and why. No claim about prevalence beyond the ten firms.
