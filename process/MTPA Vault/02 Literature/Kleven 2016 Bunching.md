## What it does
Review of bunching estimation: agents cluster where the choice set changes abruptly – at **kinks** (slope changes) or **notches** (level jumps) – and the excess mass reveals behavioural responses. Originated in tax research, since applied to social security, welfare, education, regulation and pricing. Covers estimation methods, strengths and limitations, and why kinks and notches are common in policy design.
