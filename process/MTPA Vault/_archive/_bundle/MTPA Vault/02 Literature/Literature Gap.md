Back to [[Literature Overview]] · [[Research Question]] · [[Empirical Design]]

## The gap as stated by the project
Direct effects of the Three Red Lines on **leverage, bond spreads and performance** have been studied ([[Ma and Xie [number withheld]]], [[Zheng Dong Jiang SSRN]], [[Shi Hao Xie Kim [number withheld]]]), as have **spillovers** ([[Chen Du Ma [number withheld]]], [[FRL [number withheld] Spillover Volatility]]). Whether the thresholds were **circumvented** – off-balance-sheet shifting or window dressing – has, according to the search so far, **not been studied systematically**. Evidence so far comes only from the financial press ([[Press Evidence Off-Balance-Sheet Debt]]).

Contribution in one line: *bunching at the thresholds + dose-response DiD on off-balance-sheet indicators + adjusted vs. reported leverage* → real vs. cosmetic deleveraging.

## Implications for the gap claim
- The gap claim **still holds for bunching at the thresholds and for off-balance-sheet shifting (JV, 明股实债, perpetuals)**, based on the evidence so far.
- It **does not hold in the broad form** "nobody studied window dressing by developers": Ba, Huang & Zhang ([number withheld]) measure liability window dressing. The proposal should cite it and position against it (their measure is general and pre-dates/ignores the thresholds; ours exploits the threshold design).
