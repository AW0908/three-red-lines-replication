# Press Evidence: Off-Balance-Sheet Debt

Back to [[Literature Overview]] · [[Literature Gap]] · [[Research Question]] · Outcomes: [[Empirical Design]]

So far the only evidence that the thresholds were circumvented comes from the financial press. These reports motivate the outcome variables of the DiD.

## Reuters, [number withheld] Sep [number withheld] (via The Standard, Hong Kong)
**Link:** <https://www.thestandard.com.hk/property/article/23262/Mainland-developers-seek-to-dodge-three-red-lines> ("Mainland developers seek to dodge three red lines")

Reported practices:
- **Off-balance-sheet joint ventures** to keep project debt off the consolidated balance sheet.
- **Debt disguised as equity** (明股实债, "nominal equity, real debt"): investors enter as equity holders but receive a **guaranteed return** and a **buy-back commitment**.

→ Maps to outcomes: JV/associate investments (长期股权投资), minority equity (少数股东权益).
