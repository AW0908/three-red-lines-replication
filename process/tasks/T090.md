## Logik der Indikatoren (vor Datenblick festgelegt)

Debt-like partners receive a fixed or guaranteed return and do not share project risk. Observable traces:
[number withheld]. **Profit-share gap**: MI share of total equity minus MI share of net profit (ni_mi / ni). Persistent large positive gap =
   partners hold equity but receive little of the profit (return paid elsewhere, e.g. via shareholder loans or buybacks).
   Precedent: 国金证券 ([number withheld], via [number withheld]世纪经济报道) flags gaps above [number withheld] pp.
[number withheld]. **Minority payout ratio**: dividends paid to minority shareholders (cash flow, SUBSIDIARY_PAY_DIVIDEND) / minority profit
   (ni_mi), and / lagged MI. Payouts far above minority profit, stable regardless of profit = fixed-return pattern.
[number withheld]. **MI growth without cash**: ΔMI minus minority profit minus cash received from minority investors
   (SUBSIDIARY_ACCEPT_INVEST). Large unexplained increases = consolidation or reclassification rather than new capital.
[number withheld]. **Implied financing cost**: (interest expense incl. capitalised interest, where reported) / average interest-bearing debt.
   Unusually high rates relative to peers = costs of financing not shown as debt.
[number withheld]. ~~Reporting-date pattern~~ — removed (PI [number withheld]., after feasibility stop of the first run): requires dated
   within-period transactions; the cash-flow data contain statement-period totals only. No substitute proxy
   (e.g. H1 vs. H2 contrast). Deferred as a possible extension of T089 (dated capital injections in announcements/prospectuses).

Each indicator is a red flag, not evidence. Report all, including those that show nothing.
