## Goal

Pilot of the transaction-level evidence for Q2/"Part III" (STATE 2026-10-23): for [number withheld] purposively selected developers (`state/handcollect/pilot_purposive.csv`), extract from their own reports where minority interests sit, how much cash minority shareholders contributed, what they earned, and whether contracts with debt-like features are disclosed. Every value carries document and page. This is data entry from public documents, not analysis. The PI verifies each row against the PDF afterwards (`verified_by_PI`), and does the registry lookups of shareholder identity himself.

## Inputs (read only)

- `Daten/annual_reports/<firm_id>/*.pdf` ([number withheld] files, manifest in `Daten/annual_reports/manifest.csv`)
- `state/handcollect/PROTOCOL.md` (field definitions, search terms), `state/handcollect/pilot_purposive.csv`
- `replication/data/processed/panel_firm_halfyear.csv` (MI balances for reconciliation only)

## Write scope

- `Daten/annual_reports/<firm_id>/txt/` (page-split text dumps; `pdftotext` per page, file name `<report>_p<NNN>.txt`)
- `replication/data/raw/manual/handcollect/` (new folder): `nci_subsidiaries.csv`, `nci_cashflow.csv`, `contract_hits.csv`, `rl1_published.csv`, `registry_todo.csv`, `extraction_log.md`

## Steps

[number withheld]. Text dumps: for each PDF, `pdftotext -f p -l p` page by page, into txt/. Record the page count and check it against `pdfinfo`. PDF page numbers are the reference; also record the printed page number where visible.
[number withheld]. **`nci_subsidiaries.csv`**, one row per subsidiary × report, from the table of material non-wholly-owned subsidiaries:
   - A-shares (CAS [number withheld]): 重要的非全资子公司 / 重要非全资子公司的主要财务信息;
   - HK (HKFRS [number withheld]): "subsidiaries with material non-controlling interests".
   - Columns: firm_id, report, pdf_page, subsidiary_name (as printed), nci_share_pct, profit_to_nci, dividends_to_nci, nci_balance_end, currency_unit, plus the summary financial info if printed (total assets, total liabilities, revenue, net profit of the subsidiary).
   - Reconciliation: also extract total NCI from the consolidated balance sheet (page) and compare with panel `mi` at that date. Report the difference; do not adjust.
[number withheld]. **`nci_cashflow.csv`** per report, from the consolidated cash flow statement:
   - A-shares: 吸收投资收到的现金 and 其中：子公司吸收少数股东投资收到的现金;
   - HK: "capital contribution(s) from non-controlling interests/shareholders", plus acquisitions of NCI and payments to NCI if shown;
   - with pdf_page.
[number withheld]. **`contract_hits.csv`**: search all pages for the terms in PROTOCOL.md: 明股实债, 固定收益, 保底, 回购承诺, 股权回购, 差额补足, 财务担保, 其他权益工具, 永续; English: put option, redemption, repurchase obligation, guaranteed return, financial guarantee, written put, NCI classified as financial liability.
   - For each hit: firm_id, report, pdf_page, term, a verbatim excerpt of at most [number withheld] characters, and a classification of the context: `nci_related` / `guarantee_to_subsidiary_or_jv` / `share_buyback_or_incentive` (irrelevant) / `other`.
   - Do not interpret beyond that classification.
[number withheld]. **`rl1_published.csv`**: any value the firm itself publishes for the liability-to-asset ratio excluding advance receipts (剔除预收款后的资产负债率 / "liability-to-asset ratio excluding receipts in advance"), net gearing and cash-to-short-term-debt, with date, pdf_page and verbatim wording. Add the constructed RL1 from the panel for the same date and the difference. If none is found, write one row "not disclosed" per report.
[number withheld]. **`registry_todo.csv`**: subsidiaries whose NCI balance is at least [number withheld]% of the firm's total NCI, or whose NCI balance rose by at least [number withheld]bn RMB/HKD between the two reports. Columns: firm_id, subsidiary_name, nci_balance_end, change, and a field left empty for the PI (holder names, holder_type, entry date, source).
[number withheld]. `extraction_log.md`: per report, the pages searched, the tables found or not found, and any ambiguity (unreadable tables, scanned pages, split rows). Rows that cannot be read reliably go into the log, not into the CSVs.
