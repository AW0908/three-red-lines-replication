\textit{Notes:} All six prespecified specifications are retained without significance stars. C0: baseline. C1: size and its missing indicator times post. C2: all included continuous controls and their missing indicators times post. C3: C2 plus SOE categories and market times post. C4: the C3 control set times each date, reference June 30, 2020. C5: C3 excluding 12 firms with any post-policy date having nonpositive total equity, minority equity above total equity, or RL2 above 5 (D22). This post-period exclusion is a sensitivity analysis and can select on policy outcomes. Control df counts retained control coefficients; residual df is N less all estimated parameters including fixed effects. The CSV additionally reports cluster-t inference df, within R-squared, and every requested slope term absent from the estimated coefficient vector, including all-zero missing indicators. Constant or redundant missing-indicator interactions are collinear and omitted. For RL1, the larger coefficient magnitude under C2 through C4 coincides with a conditional pre-trend: the joint pre-test under controls times date has p = 0.000223. It must not be interpreted as a stronger policy effect. C4 uses 109 slope parameters for 124 clusters (122 for minority share); the controlled event studies use 120 slopes for 124 or 122 clusters. These counts approach the number of clusters, making CR1 standard errors and joint tests unreliable. For JV over assets, C4 uses 106 slopes for 101 clusters; its CR1 standard error is displayed as --. The CSV preserves the unmodified model standard errors, intervals, and p-values for audit only in these specifications. Controls are pre-period means from 2017H1 through 2019H2 interacted with time, chosen before estimation. Contemporaneous controls are excluded because the policy can itself change these balance-sheet variables. Continuous controls: log assets, annual ROA, cash over assets, inventory over assets, and annual revenue over assets. Each requires at least three dates; observed firm means are standardized across the 124 baseline firms, then missing values are set to zero with a separate missing indicator. June income statements cover six months and December statements twelve months in both EastMoney and Wind. ROA and revenue intensity therefore use December annual reports only, with at least three annual dates. Actual and mapped report dates are at or before December 31, 2019; 2 mapped rows with later actual dates are excluded from control construction. Ownership uses the prescribed fixed panel SOE category (0, 1, or missing) and market uses A-share or HK. The inherited ownership snapshot is June 2020 with some later disclosure coding (D9 and D18); its pre-2020 status cannot be verified from this panel. Log assets retain reported currency units; unresolved HK currencies limit comparability (D7). Firm and half-year fixed effects; standard errors clustered by firm, with default small-sample corrections. Identification requires conditional parallel counterfactual trends across exposure levels, no anticipation, and no remaining dose-correlated shocks. Pre-trend tests do not establish these assumptions; both exposure and the pre-period control means overlap the pre-test window (I9).
